# Technical Roadmap

Evaluate roadmap items by value, technical urgency, dependencies, risk reduction, effort, and delivery sequencing.

Separate:
- committed technical work;
- recommended investments;
- technical debt;
- enabling work;
- risks and dependencies.

Output: priority, rationale, dependencies, risks, target window, and evidence.
