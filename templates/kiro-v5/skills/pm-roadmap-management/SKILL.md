# Roadmap Management

1. Identify roadmap initiatives and their current state.
2. Compare planned versus actual progress.
3. Check milestones, dependencies, blockers, scope changes, and forecast.
4. Flag at-risk or delayed items with evidence.
5. Produce concise status and next actions.

Preferred status: On Track / At Risk / Delayed / Blocked / Done.

Never invent dates, progress, or dependencies that are not evidenced.
